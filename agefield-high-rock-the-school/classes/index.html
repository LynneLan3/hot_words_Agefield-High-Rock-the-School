Agefield High Classes & School System Guide

Agefield High uses a Time & School System. On weekdays you attend class until the afternoon, or skip class to explore town. Official materials list five lesson types — English, Math, Geography, German, and Music — and say good grades earn pocket money.

How Does the School System Work?

Official Steam copy describes a “complex Time & School System.” On weekdays, you are expected to be in class until the afternoon. You can still skip class and do other things instead.

Publisher materials also describe attending classes through interactive mini-games. Exact mini-game rules, grade thresholds, and attendance penalties are not officially published.

All Five Classes in Agefield High

Official feature lists confirm these five lesson types:

  • English
  • Math
  • Geography
  • German
  • Music

No official source currently ranks these classes, lists unique rewards per subject, or publishes question banks.

What Is the Agefield High School Schedule?

What is confirmed: weekday classes, lasting until the afternoon.

A full daily timetable — including when each lesson starts and ends — has not been verified yet. This page will be updated after launch if a reliable in-game schedule is documented.

Can You Skip Class in Agefield High?

Yes. Official store text says cool kids skip class and do whatever they want. Skipping is tied to exploring the open world and taking on side content.

Official copy also warns you to watch out for teachers and the cops if you break the rules. Specific detention rules, wanted-level numbers, or skip penalties are not officially confirmed.

What Do Good Grades Do?

Official descriptions say you can earn pocket money by getting good grades. That makes class attendance one of the confirmed money paths, alongside side missions and activities. See How to Make Money in Agefield High for the money overview.

Exact payouts, grade cutoffs, and whether every class pays the same amount are not verified.
