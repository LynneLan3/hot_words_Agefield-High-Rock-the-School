Agefield High Map & Locations Guide

Official materials describe Agefield as a small, handcrafted open world with four confirmed area types: the school campus, two neighborhoods, the town center, and the countryside.

Quick Map Overview

World type Small, handcrafted indie open world
Confirmed areas School campus, two neighborhoods, town center, countryside
Official size Not published — no km² figure
Interactive map Not available / not verified on this site

How Big Is the Agefield High Map?

Steam and the official “Who Should Attend Agefield High?” announcement describe a smaller world with personality — not a massive AAA map. No official square kilometer size has been published, so this page does not invent one.

Agefield High School Campus

The school campus is a confirmed exploration area and the home of the Time & School System. Weekday classes run here until the afternoon. Interior layout, room names, and a school floor plan are not officially mapped yet.

See Agefield High Classes & School System for attendance and lesson types.

The Two Neighborhoods

Official Steam copy lists “two neighborhoods” as part of the open world. Developer making-of posts also show the town of Agefield being built out over time. Official names for those two neighborhoods are not published, so this guide does not invent district names.

Town Center

The town center is a confirmed area. Official press mentions a small-town sandbox with places such as bike shops, tattoo parlors, corner stores, and arcades as types of locations in Agefield. Exact storefront coordinates are not verified.

Countryside

The countryside is a confirmed area. The official Steam announcement also mentions woods behind the school in the context of country bikes for off-roading. Trail maps and countryside POIs are not officially listed.

Shops and Known Location Types

Official materials confirm you can spend money on bikes, clothes, hairstyles, tattoos, and accessories. Press copy also refers to bike shops and tattoo parlors as place types in the world.

This page does not place those shops on a map. Shop prices and NPC positions are not verified. For what cash buys, see How to Make Money in Agefield High.

Collectibles and Secrets

The official Steam announcement says that outside classes and story missions you will find collectibles and secrets to discover. That confirms they exist.

Total counts, exact locations, and a collectible map are not verified. This page will not publish a guessed secret list.

Is There a Full Interactive Map Yet?

No. This site does not currently host a verified, complete interactive map. A fake map would be worse than no map. After launch, this page can add confirmed landmarks as they are checked.
