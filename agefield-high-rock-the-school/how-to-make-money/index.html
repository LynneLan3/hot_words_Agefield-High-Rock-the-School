How to Make Money in Agefield High

Official materials currently confirm two money paths in Agefield High: Rock the School:

  1. Get good grades at school to earn pocket money.
  2. Complete side missions and activities for cash.

Exact payouts, cooldowns, and a fastest farm method are not verified yet.

Quick Answer

Go to class for steady pocket money, or skip class to chase side jobs such as lawn mowing and food delivery. Cash is spent on bikes, clothes, hairstyles, tattoos, and accessories. Which path is faster is still untested.

Earn Money by Getting Good Grades

Steam store copy says you can “earn some pocket money by getting good grades.” That is the confirmed school-side income path. Class list and attendance rules are covered in the Agefield High Classes & School System guide.

How much each grade pays, and whether every subject pays the same, is not officially published.

Earn Money Through Side Missions and Activities

Official feature lists include about 15 side missions/activities. Steam also frames skipping class to do side missions as the other way to earn cash — with teachers and cops as the stated risk.

Mission names, rewards, and repeatability are not verified.

Side Jobs and Mini-Games

Official press from Refugium Games lists side hustles and mini-games such as prank calling and lawn mowing to earn cash and unlock items. The official Steam announcement “Who Should Attend Agefield High?” also names food delivery jobs among things to do outside class and story missions.

Those jobs exist in official copy. Dollar amounts per job are not verified.

What Can You Buy With Money?

Official shop / customization lists include:

  • Bikes
  • Clothes
  • Hairstyles
  • Tattoos
  • Accessories

Steam also mentions other shop items. Specific prices are not officially listed. Shop types on the map are covered in Agefield High Map & Locations.
