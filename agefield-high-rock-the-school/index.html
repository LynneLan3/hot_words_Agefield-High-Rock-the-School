What is Agefield High: Rock the School?

Agefield High: Rock the School is an open-world coming-of-age adventure inspired by early-2000s teen comedy films. Players control Sam, a new transfer student at Agefield High who meets Kale and Axel on his first day. Kale has a plan to "Rock the School" and leave a legendary mark before graduation, and Sam is recruited to help pull it off.

The game combines a school attendance system with free-roam exploration of a small open world: the school campus, two neighborhoods, the town center and the countryside. The main campaign runs roughly 8–10 hours, with two different endings depending on player choices.

Agefield High Gameplay

Agefield High runs on a Time & School System: on weekdays, Sam has to attend classes such as English, Math, Geography, German and Music through short in-class mini-games, or skip class entirely to explore the open world and take on side content. Good grades earn pocket money, while skipping class and completing side missions or activities is a riskier way to make cash — watch out for teachers and the police.

Earned money can be spent on customization, including bikes, clothes, hairstyles, tattoos and accessories. The campaign centers on around 30 main missions (official sources have listed slightly different counts, between 30 and 32) plus roughly 15 side missions/activities, leading to one of two different endings based on player choices.
