What is Agefield High: Rock the School?

Agefield High: Rock the School is an open-world coming-of-age adventure inspired by early-2000s teen comedy films. Players control Sam, a new transfer student at Agefield High who meets Kale and Axel on his first day. Kale has a plan to "Rock the School" and leave a legendary mark before graduation, and Sam is recruited to help pull it off.

The game combines a school attendance system with free-roam exploration of a small open world: the school campus, two neighborhoods, the town center and the countryside. The main campaign runs roughly 8–10 hours, with two different endings depending on player choices.

Agefield High Gameplay

Agefield High runs on a Time & School System: on weekdays, Sam attends class until the afternoon, or skips class to explore and take on side content. Good grades earn pocket money. Official lesson types are English, Math, Geography, German and Music.

The open world includes the school campus, two neighborhoods, the town center and the countryside. Official shop lists include bikes, clothes, hairstyles, tattoos and accessories. The campaign centers on around 30–32 main missions plus roughly 15 side missions/activities, with two endings based on player choices.
