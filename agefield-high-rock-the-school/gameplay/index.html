Agefield High: Rock the School Gameplay

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

This page summarizes officially confirmed gameplay systems for Agefield High: Rock the School. It is a pre-release reference based on the Steam store page, official trailers, and publisher materials — not post-launch playtesting.

Open-world structure

Agefield High is built around a small, handcrafted open world. Official descriptions list:

  • Agefield High School campus
  • Two neighborhoods
  • Town center
  • Countryside

Steam also notes that the game is proudly indie-scale: detailed, but not designed as a massive AAA open world.

Time & school system

On weekdays, players attend Agefield High as new student Sam. Classes run until the afternoon, but players can also skip class to explore and pursue other activities. Official copy warns that teachers and cops will respond if you break the rules.

Five lesson types

Official materials list five different lesson types:

  • English
  • Math
  • Geography
  • German
  • Music

Getting good grades is one way to earn pocket money. Class answer keys and optimal grade strategies are not officially published and are not covered here.

Main missions and endings

The campaign centers on the “Rock the School” plan with friends Kale and Axel. Official feature lists currently cite:

  • About 8–10 hours of story / total gameplay duration
  • ~30–32 main missions (Steam currently says 32; some official console listings say 30)
  • Two different endings based on player choices
  • Prom includes an important choice (Steam store copy)

Side activities

Outside the main story, official materials mention roughly 15 side missions / activities. Examples cited in official press include side hustles and mini-games such as prank calling and lawn mowing to earn cash and unlock items.

Skipping class to chase side content is presented as a riskier way to make money compared with earning cash through good grades.

Combat

Official Steam guidance describes combat as intentionally straightforward — “no fifty-button combo system.” Trailer narration also shows Sam using punches, leg kicks, and a shove to break defense. Detailed combat tier lists, best weapons, or meta strategies are not officially confirmed.

Shopping, bikes, and customization

Players can spend earned money at shops. Official lists include:

  • Bikes
  • Clothes
  • Hairstyles
  • Tattoos
  • Accessories
  • Other shop items (Steam also mentions dirty magazines)
