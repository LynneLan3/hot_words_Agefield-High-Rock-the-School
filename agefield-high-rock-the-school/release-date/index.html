Agefield High: Rock the School Release Date

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

Agefield High: Rock the School is scheduled to launch on PC via Steam on August 12, 2026. Console versions have been announced for later in 2026, but exact console dates are still pending.

Confirmed PC / Steam release date

PC / Steam release date: August 12, 2026

This date is listed on the official Steam store page for Agefield High: Rock the School (app ID 3562580) and has also been announced by publisher Perp Games. As of August 11, 2026, Steam still marks the game as coming soon.
