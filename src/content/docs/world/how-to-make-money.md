## How do you make money in Agefield High?

In **Agefield High: Rock the School**, you make money two ways:

1. **Attend class and earn good grades** for pocket money / allowance.
2. **Earn cash outside class** through side jobs and side activities.

Food delivery and lawn mowing are confirmed playable money activities after launch. Exact payouts for each job, and which method is the fastest, are <span class="unconfirmed">not yet reliably verified</span>.

| Money method | Evidence | What we know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Good grades | Verified in-game | Pocket money depends on grades; one German perfect score paid $20 |
| Food delivery | Confirmed post-launch gameplay | Side job used to earn cash; exact payout still unverified |
| Lawn mowing | Confirmed post-launch gameplay | Playable side activity; exact payout still unverified |
| Prank calling | Officially referenced | Exists as a side hustle / mini-game; payout not independently verified |
| Other side missions | Confirmed generally | Exact rewards and repeatability still incomplete |

**There is not yet enough verified payout and timing data to name one method the fastest money farm.**

## Grades vs side jobs

Agefield High money splits into two different paths. They are not the same loop.

### Grades

- You attend normal classes.
- Grades affect pocket money.
- One verified German perfect-score run paid **$20**.
- This path fits the regular school day.

Class mini-games and verified answer pools are covered in the [Classes](/agefield-high-rock-the-school/classes/) guide. For how allowance fits a weekday loop, see [Gameplay](/agefield-high-rock-the-school/gameplay/).

### Side jobs

- You earn cash after school, or during time spent outside class.
- Confirmed cash activities include **lawn mowing** and **food delivery**.
- That does **not** mean side jobs are proven to out-earn grades.
- This guide does not publish unverified hourly rates or farming efficiency.

Use grades when you want allowance from the school loop. Use side jobs when you want to earn cash actively outside class.

## Earn money from good grades

The in-game tutorial states that **pocket money depends on your grades**. Steam store copy frames the same school-side income path.

One verified German perfect-score class run awarded **$20**. That observation does **not** mean every subject, or every perfect score, pays a fixed $20. A full grade-to-payout table is <span class="unconfirmed">still incomplete</span>.

For subject mechanics, see [Agefield High Classes & School System](/agefield-high-rock-the-school/classes/).

## Food delivery

**Food delivery** is a playable side job / activity outside the classroom. You can use it to earn cash as part of the after-class money path.

What is confirmed:

- It exists as a post-launch side job.
- It can earn cash.
- It sits outside the grades → pocket money loop.

What is <span class="unconfirmed">not verified</span> for this guide:

- Exact payout
- Whether the job can be repeated without limit
- Refresh / cooldown rules
- Best routes

This page does not invent a job start point, NPC, map pin, or walkthrough for food delivery.

## Lawn mowing

**Lawn mowing** is a confirmed side activity / side hustle you can use to make money.

What is confirmed:

- It exists as a playable cash activity.
- It can be used as a money-making option outside class.

What is <span class="unconfirmed">not verified</span>:

- Exact payout
- How long a run takes
- Repeatability

This guide does **not** call lawn mowing the fastest money method, and it does not publish unverified locations or refresh conditions.

## Other side jobs and activities

Official materials also frame side missions and side activities as a general cash path — including skipping class to chase them, with teachers and cops as the stated risk. Feature lists mention roughly 15 side missions / activities; treat that count as official copy, not a verified in-game checklist.

**Prank calling** appears in official Refugium Games press as a side hustle / mini-game that can earn cash. That is official/reference evidence, not the same post-launch money-run confirmation level as food delivery or lawn mowing. Payout, efficiency, and a step-by-step flow for prank calling are <span class="unconfirmed">not independently verified</span> here.

Exact rewards, cooldowns, and full job lists for other side missions remain <span class="unconfirmed">incomplete</span>.

## Money-making tips for new players

1. **Do not ignore classes just to chase cash.** Grades themselves affect pocket money.
2. **If you are already out in town,** pick up confirmed side jobs such as food delivery or lawn mowing when they fit your route.
3. **Do not treat an unverified “best money farm” as fact.** Payout and timing data are still incomplete.
4. **Spend money on bikes or customization you actually need.** Shop prices are not published here as guide data.
