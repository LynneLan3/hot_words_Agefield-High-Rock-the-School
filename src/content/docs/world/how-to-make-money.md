Official materials currently confirm two money paths in **Agefield High: Rock the School**:

1. Get good grades at school to earn pocket money.
2. Complete side missions and activities for cash.

Exact payouts, cooldowns, and a fastest farm method are <span class="unconfirmed">not verified yet</span>.

## Confirmed Ways to Earn Money

| Method | Confirmed? | What we know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Good grades | Yes | Earn pocket money |
| Side missions | Yes | Official copy says skip class and earn cash by doing side missions |
| Side activities | Yes | About 15 side missions/activities are listed |
| Lawn mowing | Yes | Confirmed activity; payout unverified |
| Food delivery | Yes | Listed in the official Steam “Who Should Attend” announcement; payout unverified |
| Prank calling | Referenced | Official press lists it among side hustles/mini-games that earn cash |
| Fastest money method | Not yet verified | Needs post-launch testing |

## Earn Money by Getting Good Grades

Steam store copy says you can “earn some pocket money by getting good grades.” That is the confirmed school-side income path. Class list and attendance rules are covered in the [Agefield High Classes & School System](/agefield-high-rock-the-school/classes/) guide.

How much each grade pays, and whether every subject pays the same, is <span class="unconfirmed">not officially published</span>.

## Earn Money Through Side Missions and Activities

Official feature lists include about 15 side missions/activities. Steam also frames skipping class to do side missions as the other way to earn cash — with teachers and cops as the stated risk.

Mission names, rewards, and repeatability are <span class="unconfirmed">not verified</span>.

## Side Jobs and Mini-Games

Official press from Refugium Games lists side hustles and mini-games such as **prank calling** and **lawn mowing** to earn cash and unlock items. The official Steam announcement “Who Should Attend Agefield High?” also names **food delivery jobs** among things to do outside class and story missions.

Those jobs exist in official copy. Dollar amounts per job are <span class="unconfirmed">not verified</span>.

## What Can You Buy With Money?

Official shop / customization lists include:

- Bikes
- Clothes
- Hairstyles
- Tattoos
- Accessories

Steam also mentions other shop items. Specific prices are <span class="unconfirmed">not officially listed</span>. Shop types on the map are covered in [Agefield High Map & Locations](/agefield-high-rock-the-school/map-locations/).

## What Is the Fastest Way to Make Money?

**Not verified yet.**

There is not enough reliable post-launch data to compare:

- Fastest farming efficiency

This section is the place to update after the August 12, 2026 PC launch, once those numbers can be checked in-game.

## Agefield High Money FAQ

### How do you make money in Agefield High?

Confirmed paths are good grades for pocket money, and side missions / activities for cash.

### Is lawn mowing a job in Agefield High?

Yes. Official materials list lawn mowing as a side activity.

### Is there food delivery in Agefield High?

Yes. The official Steam announcement lists food delivery jobs. Exact payout is <span class="unconfirmed">not verified</span>.

### What is the fastest money method?

Not verified yet.
