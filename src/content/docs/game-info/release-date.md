**Agefield High: Rock the School** is scheduled to launch on **PC via Steam on August 12, 2026**. Console versions have been announced for later in 2026, but exact console dates are still pending.

## Confirmed PC / Steam release date

**PC / Steam release date: August 12, 2026**

This date is listed on the official Steam store page for Agefield High: Rock the School (app ID 3562580) and has also been announced by publisher Perp Games. As of August 11, 2026, Steam still marks the game as coming soon.

## What Time Does Agefield High Release?

Steam currently indicates an August 12, 2026 release. The precise unlock time should be checked against the live Steam availability/countdown before treating it as final.

This page does not treat third-party timestamps as an official worldwide release time.

## How Much Does Agefield High Cost?

The official Steam Community announcement *Who Should Attend Agefield High? - A Letter From Principal Carpenter* (Refugium Games) lists:

- US PC list price: **$29.99**
- Launch discount: **10%** for the first 7 days
- US discounted price: **$26.99**
- Regional pricing may vary (a regional price table is shown in that announcement)

Steam app details still show the title as coming soon, so live storefront pricing can still change at unlock. Confirm the final checkout price on Steam at launch.
