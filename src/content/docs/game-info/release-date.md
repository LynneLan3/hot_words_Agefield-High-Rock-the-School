**Agefield High: Rock the School** released on **PC via Steam on August 12, 2026**. Console versions have been announced for later in 2026, but exact console dates are still pending.

## Confirmed PC / Steam release date

**PC / Steam — Released August 12, 2026**

This date is listed on the official Steam store page for Agefield High: Rock the School (app ID 3562580) and was announced by publisher Perp Games. Steam currently lists the title as released.

## What Time Did Agefield High Release?

Steam lists the PC release date as August 12, 2026. Exact unlock times can vary by region; use the live Steam store page as the source of truth for availability in your region.

This page does not treat third-party timestamps as an official worldwide release time.

## How Much Does Agefield High Cost?

The official Steam Community announcement *Who Should Attend Agefield High? - A Letter From Principal Carpenter* (Refugium Games) lists:

- US PC list price: **$29.99**
- Launch discount: **10%** for the first 7 days
- US discounted price: **$26.99**
- Regional pricing may vary (a regional price table is shown in that announcement)

As of August 13, 2026, Steam still shows an introductory / Early Bird-style discount offer (listed as ending August 19). Confirm the final checkout price and offer window on the live Steam store page.
