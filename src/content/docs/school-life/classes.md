## Quick Answer

Agefield High has five school classes — **English**, **Math**, **Geography**, **German**, and **Music** — each with a distinct mini-game verified in post-launch gameplay:

| Class | How it works | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English | Spelling Exercise — fill in missing letters | Verified |
| Geography | Guess the Country — map highlight + three choices | Verified |
| German | Translate the Word — English → German multiple choice | Verified |
| Math | Timed arithmetic / calculation questions | Mechanism verified |
| Music | Guitar rhythm mini-game | Verified |

Grades are not cosmetic. The tutorial states that **pocket money depends on your grades**, and one verified German perfect-score run awarded **$20**. You can skip class, but the game warns you to watch out for teachers and police.

This page publishes only answers and mechanics confirmed from in-game worksheets, tutorials, and consistent gameplay footage — not guessed keys or ASR typos.

## How Classes Work

On weekdays, Sam attends classes on a schedule that changes by day. Each lesson starts at a set time; you need to reach the correct classroom beforehand. Near class time, the map helps locate the room. You can arrive late.

Classes are timed interactive worksheets or mini-games. Performance produces a grade, and better grades feed into pocket money.

You may skip class to explore or take on other activities. Skipping is allowed, but enforcement involves teachers and police — exact penalty numbers are <span class="unconfirmed">still being verified</span>.

## English Class

### How it works

English starts as a **Spelling Exercise**. You see an English word with letters missing and pick the correct letter or letter group from three options. It is a timed quiz.

  <figcaption>An English class spelling worksheet from post-launch gameplay.</figcaption>
</figure>

The exercise draws from a **word pool**, so the exact set can differ between classes or playthroughs. This is not a fixed Question 1–3 list.

### Known English Spelling Words

These words were confirmed from clear in-game worksheet screenshots (missing letters → correct fill):

| Displayed | Correct fill | Full word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `HEIG__` | HT | HEIGHT |
| `SA__ER` | UC | SAUCER |
| `MAINT__NCE` | ENA | MAINTENANCE |
| `WOR_` | D | WORD |
| `VICIS__UDE` | SIT | VICISSITUDE |
| `CONNE__CUT` | CTI | CONNECTICUT |
| `IN__NTOR` | VE | INVENTOR |
| `F__K` | OR | FORK |
| `MILLEN__M` | NIU | MILLENNIUM |

Notes from the same worksheets: a player selecting `SID` for VICISSITUDE or `NEU` for MILLENNIUM was wrong — highlighted player choices on screenshots are not treated as answers.

Other gameplay also showed these words in the English pool (observed, not paired with full missing-letter answer sheets here): individualism, existence, vacuum, human, six, communism, grateful, incident, jostle, Caribbean, separate, discriminate, beginning.

This is an **observed English word pool**, not a complete English answer database.

### Grades and rewards

English awards a grade based on correct answers (e.g. Grade B observed in one run; 100% in another). Exact English payout amounts per grade are <span class="unconfirmed">not yet fully mapped</span>.

## Geography Class

### How it works

Geography worksheets are titled **GUESS THE COUNTRY**:

1. A world map highlights one country in red.
2. Three country names appear as choices.
3. You pick the matching country.
4. Your score becomes a grade at the end.

## Music Class

### How it works

Music is different from the quiz classes. After entering Music Class, you play a **guitar rhythm mini-game**: on-screen rhythm / button prompts, timed inputs, then a grade based on performance.

One observed run finished with **Grade D**. Story scenes where Sam plays guitar outside class are not the same as Music Class.

There is no multiple-choice answer sheet for Music.

## Grades and Pocket Money

The in-game tutorial states: **Your pocket money depends on your grades.** Better school performance affects Sam’s pocket money.

Observed instance:

- German Perfect Score → **$20**

Grades are not purely cosmetic. A complete payout table for every grade in every class is <span class="unconfirmed">not yet verified</span> — do not treat $20 as a universal perfect-class payout.

## Can You Skip Class?

Yes. The tutorial allows skipping class, with the warning: if you skip class, watch out for cops and teachers.

Skipping school has an enforcement mechanic involving teachers and police. Exact punishment values (points, detention length, etc.) are <span class="unconfirmed">not yet verified</span>.

## Class Schedule

Your timetable **changes by weekday**. English, Geography, German, Math, and Music appear at scheduled times during the school week. Check the in-game schedule and map before class so you reach the right room on time.

Partially observed from gameplay (not a full verified timetable):

- **Monday** — at least two English lessons, then Music (described in one playthrough)
- **Tuesday** — Geography in the morning (one playthrough)

Full weekly timetable verification is <span class="unconfirmed">in progress</span>. A complete Monday–Friday table is not published here yet.

## Agefield High Classes FAQ

### How many classes are in Agefield High?

Five: English, Math, Geography, German, and Music.

### What classes are in Agefield High?

English (Spelling Exercise), Math (arithmetic), Geography (Guess the Country), German (Translate the Word), and Music (guitar rhythm mini-game).

### Can you skip class in Agefield High?

Yes. Skipping is allowed, but watch out for teachers and police. Exact penalty numbers are still being verified.

Classes run at scheduled weekday times and you must reach the classroom. The timetable changes by day. A full weekly schedule is not fully verified here yet.
