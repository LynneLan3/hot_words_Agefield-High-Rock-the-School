## About Agefield High

**Agefield High: Rock the School** is an open-world, coming-of-age teen comedy adventure from developer Refugium Games. Set in a fictional town in the early 2000s, it follows new student Sam as he teams up with Kale and Axel to pull off the "Rock the School" plan before graduation. Agefield High: Rock the School is now available on PC via Steam, released August 12, 2026. PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S versions have been announced for later in 2026.

Players control Sam, a new transfer student at Agefield High who meets Kale and Axel on his first day. Kale has a plan to "Rock the School" and leave a legendary mark before graduation, and Sam is recruited to help pull it off.

The game combines a school attendance system with free-roam exploration of a small open world: the school campus, two neighborhoods, the town center and the countryside. The main campaign runs roughly 8–10 hours, with two different endings depending on player choices.

The PC version is available to buy on Steam. Early playthrough choices still center on how much time to spend attending class for pocket money versus skipping class to explore and take on side missions.
